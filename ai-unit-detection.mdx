AI Unit Detection reads a floorplan image, traces the visible unit boundaries and helps match those outlines to the units already in your project. Nothing is saved until you review and apply the results.

## Before you start

Make sure that:

- The floor has a clear JPG or PNG floorplan.
- Unit boundaries and numbers are readable at the uploaded resolution.
- The units have already been imported or added when you want Planpoint to match outlines to existing records.
- Your account has at least **one AI credit**. Each floorplan detection costs one credit.

<Note>
  AI Unit Detection works best on high-contrast architectural plans. Faint scans, overlapping annotations and low-resolution images may need more manual adjustment.
</Note>

## Detect the units

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the floor">
    Go to **Content → Floors**. Find the floorplan you want to process and click **Detect units**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose a detection mode">
    Select **Best quality** for complex or crowded floorplans. Select **Faster** for a quicker, value-optimized pass on clear and simple plans.

    Turn **Include balconies, terraces and gardens** off when you only want the enclosed interior boundary of each unit.

    {/* SCREENSHOT PLACEHOLDER (Marios): AI Unit Detection setup screen showing Best quality/Faster, the balcony toggle, credit balance and Start detection. */}
  </Step>
  <Step title="Start detection">
    Confirm the credit balance and click **Start detection**. Planpoint uploads the floorplan for analysis, finds the units, cleans up the outlines and prepares matches against your project data.

    You can follow each stage in the progress window. Processing a complex floorplan can take several minutes.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review every result">
    Planpoint opens a full-screen review after detection. Check each outline and choose whether it should:

    - Update an existing unit.
    - Create a new unit.
    - Be skipped.

    Printed unit numbers are used to suggest matches where possible. Resolve any duplicate assignments before applying the results.

    {/* SCREENSHOT PLACEHOLDER (Marios): Unit detection review screen with polygons and assignment controls visible. */}
  </Step>
  <Step title="Apply and fine-tune">
    Apply the reviewed results to save them. You can then use **Redraw** on individual units to adjust any outline that needs a manual correction.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## AI credits

A detection costs **one AI credit per floorplan**. The setup screen shows the current balance and includes a **Top up** link. Failed detections and results with no usable regions are refunded automatically.

## Troubleshooting

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="I do not see Detect units">
    Confirm that the floor has a floorplan image and that AI detection is enabled for your account. Contact Planpoint support if the action is still unavailable.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Some units were missed or merged">
    Try **Best quality** with the clearest available source image. After applying the useful results, redraw any missing or inaccurate outline manually.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="The wrong unit was selected during review">
    Use the assignment control for that region to select the correct existing unit, create a new one or skip it. Nothing changes in the project until you apply the review.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Outdoor areas changed the unit boundary">
    Run detection with **Include balconies, terraces and gardens** turned off when you only need enclosed interior outlines.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
