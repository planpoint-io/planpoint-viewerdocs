The **Media Gallery** allows you to upload many assets (such as images and plans) and apply them to many units at once.

<Info>Looking to let visitors pick between finish packages (kitchen, bathroom, etc.)? That lives in the **Custom finishes** sub-tab, see the [Choice of Finishes](/custom-finishes) guide.</Info>

## Applying assets to units

1. Go to **Content → Media** and open a sub-tab (**Gallery**, **Layouts**, **Floorplans**, **PDFs** or **Brochure**).
2. Click the assets you want. Each selected asset shows a number: that's the order it will be applied in.
3. Click **Apply**, pick the units (or floors) and click **Confirm**.

By default, the assets are **added** after the unit's existing ones. Turn on **Replace** to swap out the unit's current assets instead.

## Reordering photos

The order of a unit's photos is the order visitors see them in the viewer.

### For one unit

1. Go to **Content → Units** and click the gallery icon on the unit (or the layout icon for layouts).
2. Hover a photo and drag it by the handle in its top-left corner.

The new order is saved automatically. Variants work the same way from their **Gallery** button.

### For many units at once

1. In **Content → Media → Gallery**, click the photos in the order you want.
2. Click **Apply**. You can still drag the thumbnails at the top to adjust the order.
3. Turn on **Replace**, pick the units and click **Confirm**.

Every selected unit gets exactly that set of photos, in that order.

<Note>The order of the Media tab itself doesn't affect the viewer. Only the order inside each unit does.</Note>
